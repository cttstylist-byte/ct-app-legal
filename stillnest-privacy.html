StillNest — Privacy Policy

Effective 30 July 2026 · Last updated 30 July 2026

StillNest is a sleep, meditation and general-wellness app operated by Christian Tchino ("we", "us"). StillNest requires no account and no sign-in.

Stays on your device — always

The following never leaves your phone. We do not receive it, transmit it, or store it:

  • Your age, height, weight and sex — these are used only to calculate your daily calorie target, and that calculation runs entirely on the device
  • Your food log and calorie history
  • Your workout plan and completed exercises
  • Your streaks, mindful minutes, session history and milestones

Because there is no account, this data exists only in your app's local storage.

Sent off your device — only when you use the AI coach

StillNest includes an optional AI coach on the Fitness tab. When — and only when — you send it a message, the following is transmitted to our backend so a reply can be generated:

SentNot sent
The message text you type
Your fitness goal (lose / tone / strength)

These requests carry no identifier that ties them to you, because StillNest has no accounts. If you never open the AI coach, StillNest makes no network requests that contain your data.

What we never do

  • We do not sell your data.
  • We do not show ads, and the app contains no advertising SDK.
  • We do not use analytics or tracking SDKs.
  • We do not build a profile of you across apps or sessions.

Not medical advice

StillNest's calorie targets, workout plans and coach replies are general-wellness estimates. They are not medical, dietetic or clinical advice, and should not be used to diagnose or treat any condition. Consult a qualified professional before starting a new exercise or nutrition programme, particularly if you have an existing health condition.

Deleting your data

Because there is no account and no server-side profile, uninstalling the app permanently deletes everything StillNest stored about you. There is nothing left on our side to delete. For questions, email cttstylist@gmail.com.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes materially, we will update the effective date above and, where the change affects what is collected, notify users in the app.
