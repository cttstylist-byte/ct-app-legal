SafeNest — Privacy Policy

Effective 30 July 2026 · Last updated 30 July 2026

SafeNest is a family-safety app operated by Christian Tchino ("we", "us"). This policy explains exactly what the app collects, why, and what we never do with it.

What SafeNest collects

DataWhenWho can see it
Name, role (parent or child) and family membership When you create or join a family Members of your own family only
Location (precise) Only when you tap a check-in or send an SOS — not continuously, and not in the background Members of your own family only
Messages When you send a message in family chat Members of your own family only
Routine and task activity, device battery level When routines are completed or a check-in is sent Members of your own family only

Location, specifically

SafeNest is not a continuous tracking app. Location is read and shared only at the moment you deliberately tap a check-in or an SOS button. The app does not collect location in the background, and children can always see what has been shared and with whom.

What we never do

  • We do not sell your data.
  • We do not share it with advertisers, data brokers, or any third party for marketing.
  • We do not use it for advertising, profiling, or cross-app tracking.
  • SafeNest contains no advertising SDKs and shows no ads.

Children

SafeNest is designed as a parental tool. A family is created by a parent, and a child joins only by entering an invite code the parent gives them. Data about a child is collected solely to provide the family-safety service to that child's own family, under parental supervision and consent. There is no public content, no discovery of other users, and no way for a stranger to contact a child through the app.

Where the data lives

Data is stored using our infrastructure provider, Supabase, and is encrypted in transit. Access is restricted by row-level security so that one family cannot read another family's data.

Deleting your data

You can delete your family and all of its associated data at any time from within the app, or by emailing us at cttstylist@gmail.com. We will action deletion requests within 30 days.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes materially, we will update the effective date above and, where the change affects what is collected, notify users in the app.
